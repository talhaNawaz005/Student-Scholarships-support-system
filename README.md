SUMMARY:  
The Student Scholarship Support System is a web-based platform that aims to assist students in finding suitable international scholarship opportunities, universities and academic supervisors. Today, scholarship information is scattered across many sources, making it challenging for students to locate relevant opportunities and submit applications on time. The proposed system will provide a one-stop platform for students to get information about the scholarship, eligibility criteria, application requirements, deadlines and university details. The system is also expected to help students with AI assisted features such as scholarship recommendations, supervisor matching and application document assistance.  The system will make the process of searching for and applying for scholarships easier. The aim is to improve access to opportunities for international education and reduce missed opportunities due to a lack of guidance and information.
